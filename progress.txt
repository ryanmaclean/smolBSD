## Codebase Patterns
- Coordinator per-task halt behavior is governed by `halted_tasks` plus `HALT.<task_id>` markers; dispatch skips tasks listed in `halted_tasks`.
- Resume actions are carried via spool headers `X-Resume-Tag` and `X-Resume-Action` and must remove both state and per-task marker for retry/edit.
- Global halt (`var/mail/HALT`) and per-task halt (`var/mail/HALT.<task_id>`) are separate controls; per-task resume should not require global halt.
- Nushell tests are authoritative for coordinator FSM behavior: `tests/coord-tick-test.nu` and `tests/mbox-parse-test.nu`.
